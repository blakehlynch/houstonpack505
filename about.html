About & Activities

Who we are, how the pack works, and what we do each year.

Who We Are

Pack 505 has served families in the West University, Southgate, and Medical Center areas for over 30 years. Scouts come from St. Vincent de Paul Catholic School, Roberts Elementary, The Rice School, and nearby schools. Families of all faiths and backgrounds are welcome.

Our chartered organization is St. Vincent de Paul Catholic Church, home to Scouting for over 80 years. When scouts finish 5th grade, many continue in Troop 505 (boys) or Troop 506 (girls).

How the Pack Works

Dens

Scouts are grouped by grade into dens of about 5–10 kids, usually two dens per grade. Dens meet 1–2 times a month at times set by each den's families.

Pack Meetings

The whole pack meets on the 1st Thursday of each month at 6:30 PM at St. Vincent de Paul for awards, guest speakers, and activities.

Parent Volunteers

We are an all-volunteer pack. Parents lead dens and run events, and we help new volunteers get trained. See the Leaders page.

Pack Activities

Family Camping

Several family campouts a year at state parks and Scout camps within driving distance of Houston. Beginner-friendly, and siblings are welcome.

Pinewood Derby

Each January, scouts build and race their own wooden cars on the pack's track.

Blue & Gold Banquet

Our February celebration of Scouting's birthday, with awards and the Arrow of Light crossover ceremony.
