About Pack 505

Over 30 years of Scouting traditions, character development, and family adventures in Southwest Houston.

Our History & Heritage

A Proud 80+ Year Scouting Legacy at St. Vincent de Paul

Pack 505 is an active, adventurous group of families and students drawn from several schools in the West University, Southgate, and Texas Medical Center areas of Houston—including St. Vincent de Paul Catholic School, Roberts Elementary School, The Rice School, and surrounding neighborhoods.

Pack 505 has been a continuously operating Cub Scout pack for over 30 years. Along with our partner troops—Scouts BSA Troop 505 (for boys) and Scouts BSA Troop 506 (for girls)—we are part of a proud Scouting legacy at St. Vincent de Paul Catholic Church (our chartered organization) that spans over 80 years!

We are one of the larger and most vibrant packs in the Sam Houston Area Council (Twin Bayou District), with active membership averaging 90 to 100 Cub Scouts each year.

The Scout Oath
"On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight."
The Scout Law

A Scout is Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly, Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful, Thrifty, Brave, Clean, and Reverent.

The Cub Scout Motto: "Do Your Best!"

Pack Dens Structure

Each pack is organized into school-year based Dens:

  • Lions: Kindergarten
  • Tigers: 1st Grade
  • Wolves: 2nd Grade
  • Bears: 3rd Grade
  • Webelos: 4th Grade
  • Arrow of Light (AOL): 5th Grade

Due to our pack size, we typically have 2 dens per rank, with 5–10 scouts per den.

Meetings & Schedule

Pack Meetings: Held on the first Thursday of each month at 6:30 PM in the St. Vincent de Paul Family Center or School Cafeteria (6802 Buffalo Speedway). All dens, scouts, and families attend together for awards, guest speakers, rocket launches, and science demonstrations.

Den Meetings: Dens meet 1–2 times per month on days and locations selected by the parents and leaders of each den.

Parental Involvement

An All-Volunteer Community

Cub Scouts is designed to be hands-on, and parents play an essential role in delivering a vibrant program. From coordinating meeting activities and helping with pinewood cars to serving as Den Leaders, Assistant Cubmasters, or event coordinators, there are many fun ways to get involved.

We are an all-volunteer pack—we succeed because parents like you step forward. We provide full training, mentoring, and support for all volunteer roles!

Membership Fees & Funding

Transparent Dues & Financial Assistance

Scouting is designed to be affordable for every family. Here is a clear breakdown of annual costs:

$85 +$6 ins.
National BSA Registration

Annual membership paid directly to Scouting America upon online registration.

$100
Pack 505 Annual Dues

Covers campsite fees, rank belt loops, Pinewood Derby car kits, neckerchiefs, slides & supplies.

$15
Scout's Life Magazine

Optional 1-year subscription to the award-winning official youth magazine (highly recommended).

🌟 Financial Assistance Policy:

Funding should never be a reason a child cannot participate in Scouting. If your child wants to join but your family is experiencing financial hardship, Pack 505 and our charter organization will work it out. Please reach out confidentially to our Cubmaster or Committee Chair.
