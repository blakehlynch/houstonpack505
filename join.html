How to Join

Registration is Quick, Easy, and Open Year-Round!

Pack 505 is open to boys and girls in Kindergarten through 5th Grade. While we primarily recruit from St. Vincent de Paul Catholic School, Roberts Elementary, and The Rice School, we welcome families living anywhere across the Houston metropolitan area.

⭐ 3 Easy Steps to Register:

1
Complete Online Registration: Click the button below to register directly with Scouting America under Pack 505 (Sam Houston Area Council / Twin Bayou District).
Official BSA Online Application ↗
2
Receive Den Placement: Our Pack Leadership will reach out to welcome you and connect you with your scout's grade-level Den Leader.
3
Get Uniform & Attend Your 1st Meeting: Pick up a handbook and uniform at the Houston Scout Shop and join us at our next Pack Meeting!
