Join Pack 505

Join Pack 505

Open to boys and girls in Kindergarten through 5th grade. Registration is open year-round.

How to Join

  1. Register online with Scouting America using the button below. It's already set up for Pack 505.
  2. Get placed in a den. A pack leader will contact you and connect you with your scout's den leader.
  3. Come to a pack meeting on the 1st Thursday of the month at 6:30 PM at St. Vincent de Paul.
Register Online ↗ Email Us First

Cost

  • Scouting America registration: paid when you register online. See the current fee at Cost of Cub Scouting ↗
  • Pack 505 dues: $100 per year. This covers campsite fees, Pinewood Derby kits, awards, and supplies.
  • Uniform and handbook: available at the Scout Shop ↗

Cost should never keep a child out of Scouting. If you need financial help, email packleaders@houstonpack505.org. All requests are confidential.

Common Questions

Can girls join?

Yes. Pack 505 has dens for both boys and girls.

Do we need to attend St. Vincent de Paul?

No. St. Vincent de Paul is our chartered organization and meeting place, but families of all faiths and schools are welcome.

How much time does it take?

About one pack meeting and 1–2 den meetings a month, plus optional weekend events and campouts.
