Welcome to Pack 505

A Welcoming, High-Adventure Community for Houston Families

Pack 505 is an active, adventurous group of families and students drawn from several schools in the West University, Southgate, and Medical Center areas of Houston—including St. Vincent de Paul Catholic School, Roberts Elementary School, The Rice School, and surrounding neighborhoods.

Cub Scouting is a year-round, family-centered program that offers exciting hands-on activities promoting physical fitness, character, mental alertness, and moral leadership. Whether we're launching model rockets, car camping under Texas stars, carving Pinewood Derby cars, or serving our local community, there's always an adventure waiting!

Scouting by Grade

Explore the Cub Scout Dens (Grades K – 5)

Every child belongs to a small, grade-specific group called a Den (typically 5–10 scouts), led by parent volunteers. Select a rank below to see what your scout will experience!

Kindergarten • Age 5-6

Lion Den: First Steps into Adventure

The Lion program introduces kindergarteners and their adult partners to the excitement of Scouting. Activities focus on making friends, exploring nature, learning cooperation, and building confidence through simple, energetic games and outdoor play.

🎯 Key Adventures: Lion's Honor, Fun on the Run, Mountain Lion, King of the Jungle.
🤝 Parent / Akela Role: Adult partner attends meetings alongside the scout for meaningful 1-on-1 bonding.
🌟 Signature Highlights: Water Balloon Monsoon, beginner campout walks, crafts, and games!
Register a Lion Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
1st Grade • Age 6-7

Tiger Den: Curiosity and Discovery

Tigers take bigger steps into teamwork, family exploration, and community awareness. Scouts explore local parks, practice bike safety, discover nature, and participate in their very first Pinewood Derby!

🎯 Key Adventures: My Tiger Jungle, Tigers in the Wild, Games Tigers Play, Team Tiger.
🤝 Parent / Akela Role: Adult partner participates actively in den meetings, field trips, and campouts.
🌟 Signature Highlights: Pinewood Derby car building and family campfire s'mores.
Register a Tiger Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
2nd Grade • Age 7-8

Wolf Den: Growing Skills and Confidence

Second graders in the Wolf den practice outdoor navigation, knot tying, healthy habits, and community service. Wolves are also eligible to start earning exciting STEM Nova Awards!

🎯 Key Adventures: Call of the Wild, Council Fire, Running with the Pack, Paws on the Path.
🤝 Parent / Akela Role: Parents support Den Leaders and participate enthusiastically in overnight campouts.
🌟 Signature Highlights: Whittling basics, model rocketry, and STEM Nova science experiments.
Register a Wolf Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
3rd Grade • Age 8-9

Bear Den: Independence & Whittling Chip

Bears tackle exciting challenges including woodcrafting, knife safety (earning the famous Whittling Chip), cooking outdoors, and discovering Texas wildlife and history.

🎯 Key Adventures: Bear Claws (Knife Safety), Bear Necessities (Camping), Furry Friends.
🔪 Whittling Chip: Earn the privilege to carry and safely use a pocketknife at camp.
🌟 Signature Highlights: Outdoor camp cooking, carving soap and wood, and advanced derby designs.
Register a Bear Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
4th Grade • Age 9-10

Webelos: "We'll Be Loyal Scouts"

Webelos marks the transition from Cub Scouting to older Scout leadership. 4th graders take charge of their own adventures, lead flag ceremonies, practice first aid, and hike Texas State Parks.

🎯 Key Adventures: Cast Iron Chef, First Responder, Stronger Faster Higher, Webelos Walkabout.
🎖️ Youth Leadership: Scouts learn to plan camp meals, build fires, and mentor younger cubs.
🏅 Supernova Medal: Eligible to work with mentors for the prestigious Dr. Luis Alvarez medal.
Register a Webelos Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
5th Grade • Age 10-11

Arrow of Light: The Pinnacle of Cub Scouting

Arrow of Light (AOL) is the highest rank in Cub Scouts. Scouts prepare to graduate into Scouts BSA (Troops 505 and 506) by mastering outdoor survival, patrol leadership, and wilderness skills.

🎯 Key Adventures: Outdoor Adventurer, Scouting Adventure, Building a Better World.
🌉 Crossover Ceremony: A memorable bridge ceremony crossing over into Scouts BSA Troops.
🥉 Supernova Medal: Eligible for the Dr. Charles Townes Supernova Bronze Medal.
Register an AOL Scout → Den meetings at SVdP
Pack Traditions

What Makes Pack 505 Special?

From overnight campouts under Texas stars to high-octane Pinewood Derby races, our scouts build memories that last a lifetime.

🏕️ Family Camping

We go car camping 4-5 times per year at Texas State Parks and Scout camps within 60-90 minutes of SVdP. Perfect for beginners with flush toilets and hot showers nearby. No experience required!

Camping Guide & Checklist →

🏎️ Pinewood Derby

A legendary annual January tradition! Scouts design, carve, paint, and race their 5-ounce wooden racecars down our precision electronic track. Features speed heats and the fan-favorite demolition derby!

Derby Rules & Tips →

💦 Water Balloon Monsoon

Our explosive back-to-school kickoff event at Roberts Elementary! Thousands of water balloons, super-soakers, and popsicles. The ideal time for prospective scouts to meet friends and join the Pack.

See All Activities →

⚜️ Blue & Gold Banquet

The annual birthday banquet celebrating the founding of Scouting! Features great food, rank advancement awards, scout skits, entertainment, and the prestigious Arrow of Light graduation.

🔬 STEM Nova & Supernova

Hands-on science, robotics, coding, math, astronomy, and rocketry mentored by real scientists and engineers within our pack parent community.

Our fall fundraiser teaches entrepreneurship, goal setting, and public speaking while funding our entire program, keeping family dues low and subsidizing campouts!

The Pack 505 Difference

Why Parents in Southwest Houston Choose Pack 505

We make family scouting fun, manageable, and unforgettable for busy modern families.

🏕️

Beginner-Friendly Car Camping

Never camped before? No gear? No problem! Our experienced families will help you pitch your tent, loan spare gear, and guide you every step of the way. All campsites have flush toilets and water nearby.

🤝

Strong Community & Lasting Bonds

Scouts forge friendships across grades and schools that last all the way through high school and Eagle Scout rank in Troops 505 & 506. Parents make lifelong neighborhood friends too!

💰

Affordable & Financial Assistance

Scouting is one of the highest value youth activities in America. Through our fall popcorn drive, pack dues are kept low ($100/year). Cost is never a barrier for any child who wants to scout with Pack 505.

Get Started

Ready to Start Your Child's Scouting Journey?

Joining Pack 505 is simple! Registration is open year-round for boys and girls from Kindergarten through 5th Grade. You can register immediately online through Scouting America or send us a message with any questions.

1
Submit Online Registration: Sign up through official BSA registration at my.scouting.org.
2
Connect with your Den: You'll be introduced to your Den Leader and fellow families in your scout's grade.
3
Attend your 1st Meeting & Campout: Join our monthly Pack meeting at SVdP and get ready for adventure!
