Cub Scouts Pack 505

A Cub Scout pack for boys and girls in Kindergarten through 5th grade, chartered by St. Vincent de Paul Catholic Church in Southwest Houston for over 30 years.

How to Join About the Pack

What Is Cub Scouting?

Cub Scouting is Scouting America's program for kids in grades K–5. Scouts meet in small groups by grade (called dens), earn a new rank each year, and learn by doing.
