Pack Activities & Traditions

Explore the thrilling events, camping trips, and traditions that make Cub Scouting unforgettable.

Signature Traditions

Year-Round Adventure for the Whole Family

Cub Scouts offers an active calendar of outdoor adventures, STEM exploration, and family fun.

🏕️ Family Camping

We camp 4-5 times per year (October, December, February, April) at Texas State Parks and Scout ranches within 60-90 minutes of SVdP. This is beginner-friendly car camping with flush toilets and water nearby. Parents, scouts, and siblings are all welcome!

Camping Guide & Checklist →

🏎️ Pinewood Derby

Every January, scouts design, carve, paint, and fine-tune official 5-ounce wooden racecars. We race on an electronic 4-lane track, celebrate speed trophies, and cap off the day with our famous demolition derby!

Derby Rules & Building Tips →

💦 Water Balloon Monsoon

Our explosive back-to-school kickoff event at Roberts Elementary! Thousands of water balloons, super soakers, and ice pops. It's the ultimate summer meetup and the best time to invite friends to join Pack 505.

Held in August

⚜️ Blue & Gold Banquet

Celebrating the birthday of Cub Scouting each spring! Families enjoy a catered banquet, scout skits, entertainment, rank advancement presentations, and the Arrow of Light graduation into Troops 505 & 506.

Held in February/March

⛪ Scout Sunday Mass

Each February we celebrate Scout Sunday with a special Mass at St. Vincent de Paul Catholic Church honoring youth and adult volunteers. Scouts who have completed religious emblem programs are presented with their medals.

Religious Emblems Info →

🔬 STEM Nova & Supernova

Explore science, coding, robotics, and rocketry! Mentored by scientists and engineers within our pack parent community, scouts earn coveted Nova pins and Supernova medals.

STEM Counselors & Mentors →

🍿 Popcorn Fundraiser

Our fall fundraiser teaches entrepreneurship, money management, and public speaking. Scouts sell storefront, online, and neighborhood orders through the Trail's End app, earning prizes and funding pack campouts!

Trail's End Portal ↗

🏕️ Summer Camps

Keep the adventure going all summer! Scouts participate in SHAC Twilight Camp (evening day camp in June) and Camp Bovay Resident Camp (multi-day overnight adventures in July with archery, swimming, and BB guns).

SHAC Camp Info ↗

🤝 Community Service

"A Scout is helpful." Each year Pack 505 gives back to our Houston community through volunteer service projects, including food drives for the Houston Food Bank and conservation projects at local parks and scout camps.
