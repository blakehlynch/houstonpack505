Scout Day with Rice Owls Baseball!

Join Pack 505 and Rice Athletics for our annual Scout Day with the Rice University Owls Baseball team at Reckling Park! Scouts in uniform get to walk the field during the pregame national anthem and run the bases after the game.

Past Feature

Special Pack Meeting with US Coast Guard Chief Petty Officer Picou

Pack 505 was honored by a special visit and presentation from Chief Electronics Technician Jeremiah Picou of the United States Coast Guard. Chief Picou shared his inspiring journey from Tiger Cub to Eagle Scout, his career maintaining cutter electronics and search-and-rescue navigation, and how Scouting skills built his life's path.

⚜️ Scout Oath Photo Scavenger Hunt

Every summer, Pack 505 scouts take part in our 6-part Scout Oath Photo Scavenger Hunt:

  1. Duty to God & Country: Acts of religious expression or community service.
  2. Obey the Scout Law: Helping family, writing thank-you notes.
  3. Help Other People: Neighborhood or pack service projects.
  4. Physically Strong: Hiking, swimming, biking, camping.
  5. Mentally Awake: Puzzles, museum visits, reading.
  6. Morally Straight: Kindness to friends, siblings, and classmates.

Upload your photo collage to our Facebook group or bring it to the first fall Pack meeting to earn a special patch!
