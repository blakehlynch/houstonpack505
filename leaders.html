Leaders

Training and resources for den leaders, committee members, and parent volunteers.

Youth Protection Training is required for all registered adult leaders and must be renewed every two years. It's free, online, and open to every parent.
Take YPT on my.scouting.org ↗

Training

  • Position-specific training ↗ Online courses for den leaders, Cubmasters, and committee members, in the my.scouting.org Training Center.
  • BALOO outdoor training ↗ At least one BALOO-trained adult is required at every pack campout. See the council training schedule.
  • Wood Badge ↗ Advanced leadership training for experienced Scouters.
