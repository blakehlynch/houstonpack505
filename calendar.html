Meeting Schedules

When & Where Pack 505 Meets

Monthly Pack Meetings: 1st Thursday of every month at 6:30 PM at St. Vincent de Paul Catholic Church & School (6802 Buffalo Speedway, Houston, TX 77025). All scouts, parents, and siblings attend together!

Den Meetings: Dens meet 1–2 times per month. Meeting days, times, and locations are scheduled by the parents and leaders of each individual den to best fit family schedules.

How to Subscribe to Scoutbook Calendar Feeds

Never miss an event! You can sync your Pack and Den calendar directly to your iPhone / Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook.

📱 Apple Calendar (iPhone/Mac)

  1. Log in to Scoutbook Plus.
  2. Navigate to Calendar.
  3. Click Export / Subscribe and copy the webcal:// link.
  4. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Subscribed, and paste the URL.

🗓️ Google Calendar

  1. Log in to Scoutbook Plus.
  2. Click Calendar → Subscribe and copy the link.
  3. Open Google Calendar in a browser.
  4. Next to Other calendars, click + → From URL and paste your Scoutbook feed link.

💻 Microsoft Outlook

  1. Copy the calendar feed URL from Scoutbook Plus.
  2. In Outlook, go to the Calendar view.
  3. Click Add Calendar → Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the URL, name it "Pack 505", and save.
