Cub Scout Awards & Recognition

Recognizing individual achievement, faith development, outdoor skills, and Texas heritage.

Beyond Rank Advancement

Special Badges, Medals & Emblems

In addition to advancing through rank (Lion → Tiger → Wolf → Bear → Webelos → Arrow of Light), Pack 505 scouts can earn a wide variety of special awards.

⛪ Religious Emblems

"A Scout is Reverent." Religious groups have created faith development emblems approved by BSA for wear on the uniform. Programs are available for Catholic scouts at SVdP (Light of Christ, Parvuli Dei) and scouts of all other faiths.

All Faiths Emblems Chart ↗

🏕️ Outdoor Activity Award

Recognizes Cub Scouts who participate in outdoor activities and nature appreciation. Participating in our Pack 505 overnight campouts and attending day camp or resident camp fulfills the requirements!

Campout Schedule →

🤠 Texas Badge

Scouts earn this special patch by learning about the rich history of the Lone Star State and visiting a historic Texas site (such as the Alamo, San Jacinto Monument, or historic landmarks in Houston).

Texas Badge Requirements PDF ↗

⚔️ San Jacinto Battleground Award

Earned by visiting the historic San Jacinto Battleground State Historic Site in La Porte, TX, where Texas won its independence in 1836, touring the monument museum, and completing activity questions.

San Jacinto Award PDF ↗

❄️ SHAC Winter Camper Patch

Awarded to registered scouts and leaders who complete three days and two nights of outdoor camping when the thermometer dips to 42.5°F or lower during a Sam Houston Area Council campout.

Earned on Winter Campouts

🗣️ Interpreter Strip & Recruiter

Scouts fluent in a second language (Spanish, French, Mandarin, Vietnamese, ASL, etc.) can qualify for an official uniform Interpreter Strip. Scouts who recruit two new friends to Pack 505 earn the Recruiter Strip!
