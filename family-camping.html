Car Camping Adventure

Outdoor Fun for the Whole Family — No Experience Needed!

Camping is one of the most beloved traditions of Pack 505! We organize 4 to 5 campouts per school year (typically October, December, February, March, and April) at Texas State Parks and Scout camp properties within 60 to 90 minutes of SVdP (such as Huntsville State Park, Brazos Bend, Camp Bovay, and Stephen F. Austin State Park).

This is beginner-friendly car camping! You drive right up to our reserved group campsite. Restrooms with running water and flushing toilets (and often hot showers) are just a short walk away. Whether you're an experienced outdoors enthusiast or sleeping in a tent for the very first time, Pack 505 families are here to help you set up and make you feel at home.

Safety First

The Golden Rules of Pack 505 Campouts

  • Family & Sibling Friendly: Moms, Dads, brothers, and sisters are welcome! Each Cub must have at least one parent or adult guardian attending in camp.
  • Tent Sharing Policy: Each Cub shares a tent with their own parent or guardian. Two older Cubs of the same gender may share a tent if no adults are inside.
  • Two-Deep Leadership & BALOO: Every campout has BSA Youth Protection certified and BALOO (Basic Adult Leader Outdoor Orientation) trained leaders present.
  • Closed-Toe Shoes: Closed-toe shoes (sneakers or hiking boots) are required for all scouts, parents, and siblings. No sandals, flip-flops, or Crocs in camp!
  • Fire Safety: Campfires must be attended at all times. Fire buckets provided by the Pack must be present at every fire ring.
  • Pocket Knives: Bears, Webelos, and AOL scouts who have earned their Whittling Chip may carry and use a pocketknife ONLY under direct adult supervision.
  • No Pets Policy: Pets are strictly not permitted on BSA campouts for the safety and comfort of all families.
  • Substance-Free: Alcohol, tobacco, and vaping are strictly prohibited at all Cub Scout events.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions for New Campers

Got questions about your first campout? We've got answers!

First... relax! Pack 505 camps at Texas State Parks and dedicated Scout properties that are close to towns, have running water, flushing toilets, and hot showers. You will be camping with friendly, experienced families who will gladly help you pitch your tent, build a campfire, and show your scout the ropes. If you need to borrow a tent or gear, let your Den Leader know—we have plenty of spare gear!

Meal plans are coordinated by each individual Den before the campout. Usually, families eat dinner before arriving on Friday or bring an easy dinner/snacks. Saturday breakfast, lunch, and dinner are often planned together as a den or pack potluck. Your Den Leader will send out the exact meal schedule and assignments prior to departure.

Scouts wear their official Class A field uniform for flag ceremonies (Saturday morning & Sunday closing) and the Saturday evening campfire program. During daytime hikes, games, and camp chores, scouts wear comfortable outdoor play clothes or Pack T-shirts (Class B). It is expected and encouraged that scouts get a little dusty having fun in nature!

Bears (3rd grade), Webelos (4th grade), and Arrow of Light (5th grade) scouts who have completed knife safety training and earned their Whittling Chip card may carry a pocketknife in camp if they have their card on their person. Knives may only be opened and used under direct, 1-on-1 adult supervision for whittling or camp tasks. Lions and Tigers do not carry pocketknives.
